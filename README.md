# Barber site concepts

Three single-page website options for an English-speaking barber in Brazil whose clients are Americans.

| Concept | Look | Open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hollis & Co. | Classic dark shop | [heritage/](heritage/) |
| Atelier Corte | Editorial, black and white | [editorial/](editorial/) |
| Casa Norte | Bright, with a section for visitors from the US | [sunlit/](sunlit/) |

Each page is in English, lists prices in USD (with reais beside them), and includes a booking form. The form stays on the page and does not send yet. Casa Norte also has a WhatsApp button with a placeholder number.

The shop names, the Ipanema address, the prices, and the quotes are placeholders.
